About KnowYourSponsor

KnowYourSponsor is an independent public register directory and corporate solvency ledger. This page documents who built it, why it exists, our strict data provenance rules, and the ethical boundaries we enforce.

Who built it

My name is Roshan Yadav. I built KnowYourSponsor because public immigration data was opaque, unwieldy, and lacked critical corporate solvency context. The UK Home Office publishes the complete list of employers licensed to sponsor Skilled Worker and temporary visas as an unindexed, multi-megabyte raw table with no historical diffs, no sector classification, and zero cross-referencing with statutory company health.

For skilled migrants, visa sponsorship determines legal status and employment rights. Roughly 127,000 employers hold a licence out of approximately five million registered UK entities. Job seekers frequently invest months applying to companies that either do not hold licences, have had their licences revoked, or are in active corporate insolvency.

Direct inquiries, bug reports, and dataset corrections can be sent to knowyoursponsor8@gmail.com. Every message is reviewed personally.

What it is, and what it is not

Primary Function: KnowYourSponsor is a public register search directory and corporate diligence tool built over statutory public registers. It provides verifiable, factual records of licensing history and insolvency filings.

Not Immigration Advice: This platform does not provide immigration advice or legal counsel. UK immigration advice is strictly regulated by the Office of the Immigration Services Commissioner (OISC). Nothing on this platform assesses individual visa eligibility, computes points scores, or guarantees sponsorship feasibility. If you require legal representation or advice, consult an adviser registered with the Immigration Services Commissioner or a solicitor regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA).

Secondary Mirror Notice: While updated daily from official feeds, this platform is an independent index. For legal proceedings or formal visa submissions, always verify live standing directly on the GOV.UK Register of Licensed Sponsors.

Institutional Commitments & Ethics

  • Zero Paywalls for Job Seekers: Searching the register, viewing company warnings, inspecting salary thresholds, and checking daily licence changes is permanently free for individual job seekers.
  • Zero Tracking & Privacy First: We do not sell, broker, or rent user emails or query logs. All register searching executes locally in your browser via a client-side Web Worker. See our Privacy Statement.
  • Zero Paid Placement or Sponsored Delisting: Sponsor rankings and search results cannot be purchased. An employer cannot pay to be indexed, promoted, or have Companies House insolvency notices or DBT National Minimum Wage enforcement records removed.
  • Verifiable Provenance: Inferred data (such as SIC industry tags or fuzzy name normalisation) is explicitly labelled as inferred across all views. Methodological details are published in our Data Methodology.

Commercial Model & Sustainability

KnowYourSponsor operates with complete commercial transparency. Individual search and open data files are provided free to the public. The infrastructure is sustained by commercial intelligence feeds, longitudinal change archives, and automated compliance watchlists provided to immigration law practices, talent advisory firms, and enterprise compliance teams.

A free utility supported by clear, B2B data licensing is ethically sound and ensures that job seekers never bear the cost of public transparency.

Corrections & Data Integrity

If a company record is incorrectly joined, if an insolvency filing has been resolved upstream, or if an official correction has been published by Companies House or the Home Office, submit the details to knowyoursponsor8@gmail.com. Factual integrity takes precedence over all other platform priorities.
