Privacy

What this site collects, which is very little, and who else sees it. In plain English, because a notice nobody reads protects nobody.

The short version

Searching is completely anonymous. Nothing you type in the search box is sent anywhere. It never leaves your browser, because the whole register is downloaded and searched on your own device.

The only personal data collected is an email address, and only if you choose to give it. It is never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

What is collected

If you sign up for the weekly email

Your email address, plus the city and industry you picked, so the email can be about your area instead of the whole country. That is all. No name, no phone number, no address.

Sign-up is double opt-in: you are not subscribed until you click the link in the confirmation email. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes you from the list.

If you only search

Nothing that identifies you. The site records anonymous page views so I know which pages are worth keeping. There are no cookies, no advertising trackers and no profiling, which is also why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to.

Your browser stores one thing locally, on your own device: whether you chose the light or dark theme. That never leaves your computer and is not readable by me.

What your browser reveals to servers

Loading any page means your device asks a server for files, and that request carries your IP address. That is true of every website. The companies below see those requests.

Who else sees anything

Four services, and no others. I do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone else, and I never will. That includes the email list, which is the fastest way to destroy the only thing this site has.

Why I am allowed to hold it

Under UK GDPR there has to be a lawful basis for processing personal data. There are two here:

  • Consent, for the weekly email. You asked for it, you confirmed it by clicking a link, and you can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Legitimate interests, for anonymous page counts and server logs. Knowing which pages are used is necessary to run the site, and it cannot identify you.

How long it is kept

Your email address is kept until you unsubscribe or ask for it to be deleted, and then it is gone. Anonymous page-view counts are aggregate numbers with nothing personal in them, so they are kept indefinitely.

Your rights

UK GDPR gives you the right to ask what data I hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to get a copy of it, and to object to it being processed. There is no charge and I will answer within one month.

In practice, for almost everyone, the answer is one email address and one city. Unsubscribing removes it immediately without needing to ask.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at knowyoursponsor8@gmail.com.

If you think I have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk. You are welcome to raise it with me first, but you do not have to.

Children

Changes to this notice
