Privacy Policy & Data Handling

KnowYourSponsor is built on zero-knowledge search principles. We do not track individual queries, do not maintain user search histories, and do not sell or rent personal information to recruiters or third parties.

Core Privacy Architecture: When you search KnowYourSponsor, your query terms never transmit across the network. The complete register index executes entirely inside an isolated Web Worker on your local device. There are zero tracking cookies and zero behavioral profiling scripts.

1. Information We Collect

A. Interactive Search Terminal

Search operations are executed 100% client-side. We do not log what company names, postcodes, or salary thresholds you type. Your browser stores exactly one value in local storage (theme: 'light' | 'dark') to preserve your visual display preference across reloads.

B. Weekly Register Change Alerts (Optional)

If you choose to subscribe to our weekly change digest, we collect your email address and your chosen geographic/industry preference. We require double opt-in verification. Every newsletter contains an automated one-click unsubscribe mechanism that permanently purges your record from our delivery list.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), our processing operations rely upon:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): Applicable strictly when you explicitly subscribe to the weekly changes email digest via double opt-in.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): Basic aggregate infrastructure telemetry necessary to maintain platform uptime, security, and integrity against automated denial-of-service abuse.

4. Your Statutory Rights

Under UK data protection law, you hold the right to request confirmation of any personal data held, request complete erasure, or object to processing. Because our search platform is anonymous, the only identifiable record we hold for any user is an active newsletter email subscription.

To exercise data rights or request immediate deletion, contact Roshan Yadav directly at knowyoursponsor8@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
