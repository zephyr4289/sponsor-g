Watchlist Monitoring Architecture

KnowYourSponsor Watchlist is an automated compliance surveillance engine. It cross-references an organization's custom roster of UK employers against daily Home Office register diffs and statutory Companies House insolvency filings.

2. Signal Latency & Detection Timelines

The Home Office register is fetched and cryptographically diffed every business day at 05:30 UTC. If an employer leaves the register or is downgraded to a B-rating, the movement is flagged within 24 hours of government publication and dispatched in your audit alert.

Companies House publishes full company registry data snapshots monthly. While critical winding-up notices and dissolution proposals are reflected in gazette filings, routine accounts overdue indicators operate on the monthly snapshot reconciliation cadence.

3. Technical & Forensic Boundaries

Explicit Operational Limits:

  • Vacancy State: Holding a sponsor licence is a statutory permission, not an active hiring announcement.
  • Individual Feasibility: We monitor employer corporate standing. Individual visa suitability requires assessment by a regulated immigration adviser.
  • Disambiguation Constraints: We resolve entities with 83% direct precision. Where multiple corporate entities share an identical normalised name, we suppress warnings rather than generate false positives.

4. Plain-Text Communication Standard

Compliance alerts are delivered as standard plain text (UTF-8). We include zero tracking pixels, zero marketing redirects, and zero third-party javascript payloads. Audit dispatches can be cleanly forwarded to legal partners, HR leadership, or internal compliance committees with full cryptographic timestamp integrity.
